HydraHeart is an Android app that helps you drink more water and keep an eye on how you're
feeling, without it ever feeling like a chore. Every drink you log pours into Hydra in its own
colour. She reacts, she talks, she remembers how your week has gone, and she grows with you.

pay, and nothing you log ever leaves your phone.

# Why HydraHeart exists

I'll be honest: I built HydraHeart for me.

I've always known how much water matters. Up to 60% of an adult body is water, and losing just 1
to 2% of it is enough to dull your focus, your mood and your energy. It shows up in the small
things too, like dry lips, tired-looking skin and that foggy afternoon feeling. I always wanted to
drink more and actually reach my goal. What I struggled with was the motivation.

So I tried the hydration apps that were already out there, and I kept running into the same thing:
the features that would really have helped me were behind a subscription. A monthly fee to help
you drink water. That, more than anything else, is why HydraHeart exists. Drinking water is about
as basic as being human gets, and I don't think anybody should have to pay to be helped with it.

So I made my own. A private little app, just for me: free, with no ads and nothing to buy. And once
it was helping me, I wanted it to help other people too.

I'm Iri, a solo developer, and I build software that helps, protects and improves people's lives.
HydraHeart is the part of that about looking after yourself.

## Why the name?

**Hydra** is her name. It's short for hydration, and she's definitely got one head, not nine.
(Give her a few levels and there'll be a crown on it.)

**Heart** is what she is, and why she exists. A glass heart that fills up as you drink, in the
colours of what you've had. It's the bit that makes HydraHeart different from a counter: it cares
how you're doing, not just how much you've had.

# Your health is not a subscription

This is the one promise HydraHeart will never break.

- **Free, forever.** It will never cost anything to download or to use.
- **No ads.** Not now, and not later.
- **No subscriptions.** There's nothing to renew and nothing that runs out.
- **No paywalls.** Every feature is for everyone. Nothing is locked away for people who pay.
- **Donations are only ever optional.** The only money HydraHeart will ever accept is a donation someone chooses to give, and someone who never donates gets exactly the same app as someone who does.

If HydraHeart grows and keeping it going starts to cost me money, that's mine to deal with. I won't
hand the bill to the people I built it for, and I'd never ask anyone to pay me just so they can
keep a healthy habit. I can't stand it when apps treat their users as something to squeeze money
out of instead of people to help, and HydraHeart is never going to be one of them.

# Built for people, not profit

I don't measure HydraHeart in downloads, star ratings or numbers on a dashboard. I'm not a machine,
and that isn't the point.

What matters to me is that somebody, even just one person, uses HydraHeart and their life gets a
little better because of it. Maybe they drink more. Maybe their skin feels better, or the afternoon
slump stops being a daily thing. Maybe they're just in a better mood, because water affects that
too. If that happens for one person, HydraHeart has done what it was made for. They don't owe me
anything for it.

So everything in it is built around you feeling better, not around keeping you on your phone:

- **Kindness, never guilt.** Hydra won't shame you for a bad day. Her reminders sound like a friend checking in, not an alarm going off: "The audacity of forgetting to hydrate! (joking. drink water)"
- **A goal for your body.** Not a generic 2 litres. Your goal is worked out from your weight, how active you are and the climate you live in, because every body needs something different.
- **The truth about what you drink.** A 355 ml latte isn't 355 ml of water. It's 300 ml, minus a little for the caffeine. Every drink knows how much it really gives you.
- **How you feel matters too.** The days you feel worst are often the days you drink least. So HydraHeart asks how you are, and when things have been hard, Hydra notices and gets gentler with you.
- **Joy that makes it stick.** A habit only lasts if it feels good, so there are levels, trophies, thirty themes and little rewards for Hydra along the way.
- **Your privacy, kept.** No account, no analytics, no internet permission. What you log is yours.

# Meet Hydra

A plain tracker is easy to ignore. A counter can't ask whether you've had anything to drink today,
and it can't be happy for you when you hit your goal. I wanted HydraHeart to feel alive: something
that notices, cheers you on and gives you a gentle push in the right direction. That's Hydra. She's
there to keep you motivated, and to give you a reason to come back that has nothing to do with
guilt.

She lives on the Home screen, and she *is* your progress. Every drink you log pours into her in its
own colour. Water is blue, coffee is brown, orange juice is, well, orange, and milk is white. A big
glass of water fills her a lot more than an espresso does.

- **She has moods.** Her face goes from concerned to content to happy to ecstatic as you get closer to your goal. Go over your caffeine or alcohol limit and she gets worried, whatever your water says.
- **She talks.** What she says depends on the time of day, how far along you are, what you've just had, which limit you're pushing, your streak and the personality you picked. There are well over a thousand lines.
- **She remembers.** Hydra keeps track of the last couple of weeks: your streak, the days you missed, whether you're trending up or down.
- **She grows with you.** A necklace at Level 2, sparkles at 3, her diary at 5, a crown at 6, flowers at 7, a rainbow shimmer at 8, a butterfly at 9 and her golden legendary form at 10.

Under her you get today's total, your caffeine, sugar, calcium and alcohol, your electrolytes,
your streak and your recent drinks with a one-tap **Undo**.

**Not a Hydra person? That's completely fine.** Not everyone wants a companion, so she's optional.
**Focus Mode** takes away her face and her speech bubbles and leaves a clean, quiet tracker: just
your goal, your progress and your numbers. You can pick it on day one or switch whenever you like.

# No guilt, ever

Missing a day is normal. You were busy, you forgot, or it was one of those stressful days when you
didn't have the energy for anything at all. Everyone's life is different, and HydraHeart is built
for real ones.

A missed day doesn't undo the work you've already put in, and it doesn't send you back to zero.
Your streak starts counting again, but your level, your HP, your trophies, your best streak and
your whole history stay exactly where they were. One day off doesn't cancel out a month of
showing up.

Hydra sees it the same way. She won't tell you off. When you come back after a few days away, she's
just glad you're here. In Heartbreak Era she'll say something like *"You've been away for a few
days Mia 💔 no judgment. I'm just glad you're here. One drink?"*

No guilt. No pressure. You just pick up where you are and keep going.

# How are you feeling?

HydraHeart is a mood tracker as well as a water tracker, because the two go together. The tired,
stressed days are the ones where the water bottle gets forgotten, and they're the days you most
deserve someone checking in on you.

Once a day, open **How We Feel** and pick **Happy**, **Calm**, **Okay**, **Tired** or
**Stressed**, with a note if something's on your mind. It takes about ten seconds. As you choose,
the little Hydra at the top changes with you: full and grinning when you're happy, nearly empty and
frowning when you're stressed.

# Eight personalities

Hydra can be your **Bestie**, a **Slay Queen**, a **Soft Girl**, in her **Villain Era**, a
**Zen Master** or your **Coach**. Same Hydra, completely different voice, and it changes everything
she says.

Two more are hidden, and Hydra only offers them when she notices a pattern:

- **Cold CEO** turns up when you've been consistently brilliant. Ten days in a row at 90% or more, or a solid fortnight at 85% or more.
- **Heartbreak Era** turns up when things have been hard. A week of missed goals, or a week of low water when your moods have mostly been tired or stressed. She gets softer and more honest with you.

When one unlocks Hydra tells you herself, and you choose whether to switch.

# Logging drinks

Tap the big **+** and pick a category: Water, Coffee & Tea, Energy Drinks, Soft Drinks, Fruit
Juice, Milk & Calcium or Alcohol. There are 70 drinks in real sizes, from a 30 ml espresso shot to a
litre of water, and each one knows its caffeine, sugar, calcium, alcohol and electrolytes.

Drinks that dehydrate you (alcohol, energy drinks and the stronger coffees) tell you how much water
they'll take back before you add them. Press **+ Add** and the amount floats up out of Hydra's
heart. Hit your goal and the whole screen celebrates with you.

# A goal that fits your body

Your body isn't average, so your goal isn't a flat 2 litres. HydraHeart works it out from your
weight, how active you are and your climate, and eases off a little if you're over 65.
**Why this goal?** shows you exactly how yours was worked out. Would rather pick your own? Switch to
**Custom**. After a good streak, Hydra might suggest it's time to aim a bit higher.

You also get daily limits for caffeine, sugar and alcohol, and targets for calcium, sodium,
potassium and magnesium, adjusted for your age and gender.

# Levels, HP and 60 trophies

Everything you do earns HP: every drink, every mood check-in, every goal you hit (with a bonus for
your streak) and every trophy. HP takes Hydra through ten levels, and each one gives her something
new. Missed days never take HP away, so a quiet week never costs you a level.

There are 60 trophies to earn, from your first sip and your first coffee to a month-long streak and
a Perfect Day where you hit every goal without going over a single limit. The locked ones show how
close you are.

# Reminders that sound like a friend

A reminder should feel like someone who cares checking in, not a nag. Set as many as you like, at
the times that suit your day, and they come through in Hydra's voice. Not "Time to drink water",
but things like "I'm not saying you forgot... but you forgot" or "Your future self is texting you:
please drink water". Pick from 50 messages, switch off the ones you don't like, write your own, and
choose the sound.

# Privacy & permissions

Caring about you includes caring about your privacy. HydraHeart doesn't have the internet
permission, so it couldn't send your drinks, moods or notes anywhere if it wanted to. It asks for
four permissions, and that's all:

| Permission | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | Showing Hydra's reminders. Say no and everything else still works. |
| **Alarms & reminders** | Sending your reminders at the times you set. |
| **Run at startup** | Putting your reminders back after your phone restarts. |
| **Vibration** | The little haptic taps around the app. |

- **Everything stays on your phone,** in HydraHeart's private storage. Other apps can't read it, and uninstalling HydraHeart deletes it.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads.** There's nothing to sign in to and nothing reporting back.
- **The fonts are the one thing that comes from the internet, and HydraHeart doesn't fetch them.** Google Play services downloads them and keeps them on your phone. If they can't be fetched, the app just uses the system font.

HydraHeart is a wellbeing companion, not a medical device. If you have a health condition that
affects how much you should drink, your doctor's advice comes first.

# Behind the app

This half is for anyone curious about how HydraHeart is made. It's a native Android app written in
Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, and it runs entirely on the phone: there's no server, no account
system and no network code.

## Built with

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | Kotlin 2.2 |
| **UI** | Jetpack Compose (BOM 2024.12.01) with Material 3 and Material icons |
| **Navigation** | Navigation Compose |
| **State** | One AndroidX `ViewModel` with Compose state |
| **Storage** | Jetpack DataStore (Preferences), with lists saved as JSON through Gson |
| **Reminders** | `AlarmManager` with a boot receiver to put them back after a restart |
| **Fonts** | Playfair Display and DM Sans as Google downloadable fonts |
| **Android** | 8.0 (API 26) and up, targeting API 36 |
| **Build** | Gradle 9.6 (wrapper) and Android Gradle Plugin 9.4 |

Hydra herself is drawn in code on a Compose `Canvas`: every shape, the layered colours of each
drink, her face, her accessories and her level effects. There are no images or animation files
behind her.

## Building it yourself

**You'll need**

- Android Studio, recent enough for Android Gradle Plugin 9.4
- JDK 17 or newer (the one that comes with Android Studio is fine)
- The Android SDK for API 36
- A phone or emulator running Android 8.0 or newer

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/iri-dev/HydraHeart.git
```

Open the folder in Android Studio, let Gradle sync, pick the **HydraHeart** run configuration and
press Run. Or from a terminal in the project folder, build a debug APK:

```bash
./gradlew :hydraheart2:assembleDebug
```

It lands in `hydraheart2/build/outputs/apk/debug/`. To build and install it on a connected phone or
emulator in one go:

```bash
./gradlew :hydraheart2:installDebug
```

The project has two Android modules. **`hydraheart2` is HydraHeart**, so that's the one to build and
run. `app` is an earlier version of the interface that's no longer worked on.

## How it fits together

- **`HydraViewModel`** holds the whole app: today's drinks, your goals, HP and levels, trophies, moods, the diary and Hydra's settings. Her lines live here too, chosen by personality, time of day, progress, what you just drank and her memory of the last two weeks.
- **`HydraDataStore`** saves and loads that state on the phone. When a new day starts it files yesterday into your history and updates your streak.
- **`GoalCalculator`** turns your profile into a daily water goal, your caffeine, sugar and alcohol limits, and your calcium and electrolyte targets.
- **`NotificationHelper`** schedules reminders with `AlarmManager`, **`ReminderReceiver`** shows them in Hydra's voice, and **`BootReceiver`** reschedules them after the phone restarts.
- **`HydraHeartCanvas`** in `ui/components/HydraHeart.kt` draws Hydra from the drinks you've logged, layer by layer.
