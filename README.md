# Discover HydraHeart

## What makes it different

- **Hydra cheers you on.** A glass heart who fills up with whatever you drink, talks to you and grows with you. Prefer a quiet tracker? Focus Mode switches her off.
- **Kindness, never guilt.** A missed day never costs you your level, your HP or your trophies, and Hydra is just glad when you're back.
- **A goal for your body.** Worked out from your weight, how active you are and your climate, and it shows you exactly how.
- **Every drink counted fairly.** 70 drinks in real sizes, each with an estimate of how much water it really adds to your day.
- **Water and mood together.** A ten-second daily check-in, and a calendar of how you've been feeling.
- **Free and private.** No ads, no subscription, no account and no analytics.

## Meet Hydra

A plain tracker is easy to ignore. A counter can't ask whether you've had anything to drink today,
and it can't be happy for you when you hit your goal. Hydra can. She's there to keep you motivated,
and to give you a reason to come back that has nothing to do with guilt.

She lives on the Home screen, and she *is* your progress. Every drink pours into her in its own
colour: water is blue, coffee is brown, orange juice is, well, orange, and milk is white.

- **She has moods.** Her face goes from concerned to content to happy to ecstatic as you get closer to your goal. Go over your caffeine or alcohol limit and she gets worried, whatever your water says.
- **She talks.** What she says depends on the time of day, how far along you are, what you've just had, which limit you're pushing, your streak and the personality you picked. There are well over a thousand lines.
- **She remembers.** Hydra keeps track of the last couple of weeks: your streak, the days you missed, whether you're trending up or down.
- **She grows with you.** A necklace at Level 2, sparkles at 3, her diary at 5, a crown at 6, flowers at 7, a rainbow shimmer at 8, a butterfly at 9 and her golden legendary form at 10.

Under her you get today's total, your caffeine, sugar, calcium and alcohol, your electrolytes, your
streak and your recent drinks with a one-tap **Undo**.

**Not a Hydra person? That's completely fine.** **Focus Mode** takes away her face and speech
bubbles and leaves a clean, quiet tracker: just your goal, your progress and your numbers. Pick it
on day one or switch whenever you like.

*Why the name?* **Hydra** is short for hydration, and she's definitely got one head, not nine. (Give
her a few levels and there'll be a crown on it.) **Heart** is what she is: the part of HydraHeart
that cares how you're doing, not just how much you've had.

## How are you feeling?

HydraHeart is a mood tracker as well as a water tracker, because the two go together. The tired,
stressed days are the ones where the water bottle gets forgotten, and they're the days you most
deserve someone checking in on you.

Once a day, open **How We Feel** and pick **Happy**, **Calm**, **Okay**, **Tired** or
**Stressed**, with a note if something's on your mind. As you choose, the little Hydra at the top
changes with you: full and grinning when you're happy, nearly empty and frowning when you're
stressed.

## Eight personalities

Hydra can be your **Bestie**, a **Slay Queen**, a **Soft Girl**, in her **Villain Era**, a
**Zen Master** or your **Coach**. Same Hydra, completely different voice.

Two more are hidden, and Hydra only offers them when she notices a pattern:

- **Cold CEO** turns up when you've been consistently brilliant: ten days in a row at 90% or more, or a solid fortnight at 85% or more.
- **Heartbreak Era** turns up when things have been hard: a week of missed goals, or a week of low water when your moods have mostly been tired or stressed. She gets softer and more honest with you.

When one unlocks Hydra tells you herself, and you choose whether to switch.

## Logging drinks

Tap the big **+** and pick a category: Water, Coffee & Tea, Energy Drinks, Soft Drinks, Fruit
Juice, Milk & Calcium or Alcohol. There are 70 drinks in real sizes, from a 30 ml espresso shot to a
litre of water, and each one knows its caffeine, sugar, calcium, alcohol and electrolytes.

Each drink also has an estimate of how much water it adds to your day. A litre of water counts in
full. A 355 ml latte counts as about 283 ml, and alcohol, energy drinks and the stronger coffees show
how much HydraHeart takes off your total for them before you add them. These are the app's own
rough estimates, there to help you compare drinks. They aren't exact or medical measurements, and
how your body handles a drink depends on you.

Press **+ Add** and the amount floats up out of Hydra's heart. Hit your goal and the whole screen
celebrates with you.

## A goal that fits your body

Your body isn't average, so your goal isn't a flat 2 litres. HydraHeart works it out from your
weight, how active you are and your climate, and **Why this goal?** shows you exactly how yours was
worked out. Would rather pick your own? Switch to **Custom**. After a good streak, Hydra might
suggest it's time to aim a bit higher.

You also get daily limits for caffeine, sugar and alcohol, and targets for calcium, sodium,
potassium and magnesium.

<details>
<summary><strong>How your goals are worked out</strong></summary>

| Goal | How HydraHeart sets it |
| --- | --- |
| **Water** | 35 ml for each kg you weigh, plus 300 ml if you're lightly active, 600 ml if you're moderately active or 1 litre if you're very active. Add 600 ml for a hot climate or take off 200 ml for a cold one, then 10% less if you're over 65. It always stays between 1.2 and 5 litres. |
| **Caffeine** | Up to 400 mg a day, or 100 mg if you're under 18 |
| **Sugar** | Up to 50 g |
| **Alcohol** | Up to 16 g |
| **Calcium** | 1,000 mg, or 1,200 mg for women over 50 |
| **Sodium** | Up to 2,300 mg |
| **Potassium** | 2,600 mg for women and 3,400 mg for men |
| **Magnesium** | 320 mg for women and 420 mg for men |

If you choose "Other" for gender, the potassium and magnesium targets sit halfway between.

</details>

These numbers are general starting points for healthy adults, in line with common guidance (400 mg
of caffeine a day, for example, is the figure the EFSA and the US FDA use). They don't take
pregnancy or breastfeeding into account, and they aren't designed for children. **HydraHeart is a
wellbeing companion, not a medical device.** If you're pregnant, under 18, or have a condition that
affects how much you should drink, such as kidney or heart problems, ask your doctor what's right
for you.

## Levels, HP and 60 trophies

Everything you do earns HP: every drink, every mood check-in, every goal you hit (with a bonus for
your streak) and every trophy. HP takes Hydra through ten levels, and each one gives her something
new. Missed days never take HP away, so a quiet week never costs you a level.

There are 60 trophies to earn, from your first sip and your first coffee to a month-long streak and
a Perfect Day where you hit every goal without going over a single limit. The locked ones show how
close you are.

# Why HydraHeart exists

## The story

I'll be honest: I built HydraHeart for me.

I've always known how much water matters. Your body needs it for almost everything it does, and when
you don't drink enough you feel it. I always
wanted to drink more and actually reach my goal. What I struggled with was the motivation.

So I tried the hydration apps that were already out there, and I kept running into the same thing:
the features that would really have helped me were behind a subscription. A monthly fee to help
you drink water. That, more than anything else, is why HydraHeart exists. Drinking water is about
as basic as being human gets, and I don't think anybody should have to pay to be helped with it.

So I made my own: free, with no ads and nothing to buy. And once it was helping me, I wanted it to
help other people too.

I'm Iri, a solo developer, and I build software that helps, protects and improves people's lives.
HydraHeart is the part of that about looking after yourself.

## Your health is not a subscription

This is the one promise HydraHeart will never break.

- **Free, forever.** It will never cost anything to download or to use.
- **No ads.** Not now, and not later.
- **No subscriptions.** There's nothing to renew and nothing that runs out.
- **No paywalls.** Every feature is for everyone. Nothing is locked away for people who pay.
- **Donations are only ever optional.** The only money HydraHeart will ever accept is a donation someone chooses to give, and someone who never donates gets exactly the same app as someone who does.

If HydraHeart grows and keeping it going starts to cost me money, that's mine to deal with. I won't
hand the bill to the people I built it for, and I'd never ask anyone to pay me just so they can

## Built for people, not profit

What matters to me is that somebody, even just one person, uses HydraHeart and their life gets a

No guilt. No pressure. You just pick up where you are and keep going.

# Privacy and getting HydraHeart

## Your data

HydraHeart has no servers, no account, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't have the internet
permission, so it can't send your drinks, moods or notes anywhere itself. Everything you log is
kept in HydraHeart's private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read it.

Three things are worth knowing:

- **Your phone's own backup can include it.** HydraHeart allows Android's standard app backup. If backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include HydraHeart's data and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new phone. That copy is made and kept by Android and Google, not by me, and you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.
- **Uninstalling deletes what's on your phone,** but not a backup Android has already made.
- **The fonts come from Google Play services.** HydraHeart doesn't download them itself. Google Play services fetches Playfair Display and DM Sans and keeps them on your phone, and if they can't be fetched the app uses the system font.

## Permissions

HydraHeart asks for four permissions:

| Permission | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | Showing Hydra's reminders. Say no and everything else still works. |
| **Alarms & reminders** | Sending your reminders at the times you set. |
| **Run at startup** | Putting your reminders back after your phone restarts. |
| **Vibration** | The little haptic taps around the app. |

## Availability

HydraHeart isn't on Google Play yet, but it's on its way, and there are no downloads here in the
meantime. When it reaches the Play Store you'll be able to install it in one tap, and the link will
be right here. Until then, you can [build it yourself](#building-it-yourself).

# Behind the app

HydraHeart is a native Android app written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. It runs entirely on the
phone: there's no server, no account system and no network code.

## Built with

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Language** | Kotlin 2.2 |
| **UI** | Jetpack Compose (BOM 2024.12.01) with Material 3 and Material icons |
| **Navigation** | Navigation Compose |
| **State** | One AndroidX `ViewModel` holding Compose state |
| **Storage** | Jetpack DataStore (Preferences), with lists saved as JSON through Gson |
| **Reminders** | `AlarmManager`, plus a boot receiver that puts them back after a restart |
| **Fonts** | Playfair Display and DM Sans as Google downloadable fonts |
| **Android** | 8.0 (API 26) and up, compiled against API 36.1 |
| **Build** | Gradle 9.6 (through the wrapper) and Android Gradle Plugin 9.4 |

Hydra herself is drawn in code on a Compose `Canvas`: every shape, the layered colours of each
drink, her face, her accessories and her level effects. There are no images or animation files
behind her.

## Building it yourself

**You'll need:**

- Android Studio, recent enough for Android Gradle Plugin 9.4
- JDK 17 or newer (the one bundled with Android Studio works)
- The Android SDK Platform for API 36.1
- A phone or emulator running Android 8.0 or newer

**1. Clone the repository**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/iri-dev/HydraHeart.git
```

**2. Build it.** In Android Studio, open the folder, let Gradle sync, pick the **HydraHeart** run
configuration and press Run. From a terminal in the project folder, build a debug APK with:

```bash
./gradlew :hydraheart2:assembleDebug
```

It lands at `hydraheart2/build/outputs/apk/debug/hydraheart2-debug.apk`.

**3. Install it** on a connected phone or a running emulator:

```bash
./gradlew :hydraheart2:installDebug
```

Good to know:

- **Build `hydraheart2`.** That module is HydraHeart. The `app` module is an earlier version of the interface that's no longer worked on.
- **Building from a terminal only?** Gradle needs to know where your Android SDK is. Opening the project in Android Studio once sets that up, or set `ANDROID_HOME`, or add `sdk.dir` to a `local.properties` file in the project folder.

## Project layout

```
hydraheart2/src/main/
├── java/com/hydraheart2/app/
│   ├── MainActivity.kt      Start-up, the splash screen and the first-run check
│   ├── Hydraapp.kt          The app's root: onboarding or the main screens
│   ├── data/                Drinks, themes, trophies, Learn articles and the data models
│   ├── ui/screens/          One file per screen
│   ├── ui/components/       Hydra herself, her speech bubble, the level popup,
│   │                        the bottom bar and the time picker
│   ├── ui/theme/            Colours, type and the Compose theme
│   ├── ui/navigation/       The navigation graph
│   ├── viewmodel/           HydraViewModel: the whole app's state and logic
│   └── util/                Saving, the goal calculator, reminders, haptics and language
└── res/                     Strings in US English, UK English, German and Spanish, and icons
branding/                    The app icon as an SVG
docs/                        The images on this page
```

## Where to find things

Paths are inside `hydraheart2/src/main/java/com/hydraheart2/app/` unless they start with `res/`.

| If you're looking for... | It's in |
| --- | --- |
| The 70 drinks and their numbers | `data/DrinkDatabase.kt` |
| Everything Hydra says: speech, mood replies, reminders and diary entries | `viewmodel/HydraViewModel.kt` |
| How hidden personalities unlock and trophies are awarded | `viewmodel/HydraViewModel.kt` (`checkPersonalityUnlocks`, `checkTrophies`) |
| The 30 themes | `data/ThemeData.kt` |
| The 60 trophies | `data/TrophyData.kt` |
| The Learn articles and myth-busters | `data/EducationData.kt` |
| The goal maths | `util/GoalCalculator.kt` |
| How Hydra is drawn | `ui/components/HydraHeart.kt` |
| Saving, loading and the change of day | `util/HydraDataStore.kt` |
| Scheduling reminders | `util/NotificationHelper.kt`, `util/ReminderReceiver.kt`, `util/BootReceiver.kt` |
| Screen text and translations | `res/values*/strings.xml` |

## How it fits together

- **`HydraViewModel`** holds the whole app: today's drinks, your goals, HP and levels, trophies, moods, the diary and Hydra's settings. Her lines are chosen from your personality, the time of day, your progress, what you just drank and her memory of the last two weeks.
- **`HydraDataStore`** saves and loads that state. When a new day starts, it files yesterday into your history, updates your streak and checks the day's trophies.
- **`GoalCalculator`** turns your profile into your water goal, your caffeine, sugar and alcohol limits, and your calcium and electrolyte targets.
- **`NotificationHelper`** schedules reminders with `AlarmManager`, **`ReminderReceiver`** shows them in Hydra's voice, and **`BootReceiver`** reschedules them after the phone restarts.
- **`HydraHeartCanvas`** draws Hydra from the drinks you've logged, layer by layer.
